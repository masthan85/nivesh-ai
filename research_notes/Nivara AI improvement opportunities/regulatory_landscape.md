## 1. SEBI IA Regulations 2013 and RA Regulations 2014 (as amended): when does an app need IA or RA registration, and where is the education/advice line?

### Takeaway
**Who must register:** anyone who gives advice on specific securities or products for consideration, or who "holds itself out" as an adviser "by whatever name called", must register as an Investment Adviser (IA).

**Two exemptions:**
- General market comments made in good faith that name no specific security.
- Advice through mass media that is widely available to the public at large.

**Where Nia carries risk:** Nia reads a user's own holdings and explains them. Telling one person what to do with their own holdings is the textbook case of personalised advice. The exemptions do not cover it.

**December 2024 amendments:** SEBI notified these on 16 Dec 2024. They:
- created part-time IA and part-time RA categories;
- raised client caps;
- added mandatory disclosure of AI use;
- made the registered entity solely responsible for whatever its AI tools output.

### Cited Findings

**Definitions and exemptions**
- Any person "acting" or "holding itself out" as an investment adviser must register with SEBI. The definition covers anyone who holds out as an IA "by whatever name called". — [Nishith Desai Associates](https://www.nishithdesai.com/default.aspx?id=5966); [IA Regulations 2013 text (ICMAI copy)](https://icmai.in/upload/pd/SEBI-IA-Regulations-2013.pdf)
- **Exemption for general comments:** a person who gives general comments in good faith on trends in the financial or securities market, or on the economic situation, where the comments do not specify any particular security or investment product. — [ICMAI copy of IA Regs](https://icmai.in/upload/pd/SEBI-IA-Regulations-2013.pdf); [TaxTMI](https://www.taxtmi.com/article/detailed?id=11720)
- **Mass-media exclusion:** advice given through a newspaper, magazine, or any electronic, broadcasting or telecom medium that is widely available to the public at large is excluded from "investment advice". — [Nishith Desai](https://www.nishithdesai.com/default.aspx?id=5966)
- The amended definitions now cover part-time IAs (PTIAs). The amendment also reportedly excludes "reading calls" from "investment advice". — [ELP Law alert, 27 Dec 2024](https://elplaw.in/leadership/sebi-amends-and-modernises-the-investment-adviser-regulations/)

**IA (Second Amendment) Regulations 2024 (notified 16 Dec 2024)**
- **Part-time IAs:**
  - They may advise while also running another business or holding a job.
  - They are capped at 75 clients.
  - They must identify themselves as part-time in client communications.
  - Source: [Upstox](https://upstox.com/news/business-news/financial-regulations/sebi-allows-part-time-investment-advisers-puts-conditions-for-ai-use-all-you-need-to-know/article-135636); [Moneylife](https://www.moneylife.in/article/sebi-introduces-major-reforms-for-investment-advisors-and-research-analysts/75888.html)
- **Individual IAs:** the client threshold rose from 150 to 300. An individual IA with more than 300 clients, or fee income above Rs 3 crore in a financial year, must move to non-individual registration. — [Business Standard, 17 Dec 2024](https://www.business-standard.com/amp/markets/news/sebi-eases-norms-for-boarding-of-investment-advisors-research-analysts-124121701008_1.html)
- **AI disclosure (IA side):** the IA must disclose which AI tools it uses, at what stages of the advisory process, and what limitations it acknowledges in AI output.
  - SEBI circular SEBI/HO/MIRSD/MIRSD-PoD-1/P/CIR/2025/003, dated 8 Jan 2025, operationalises this.
  - The same circular clarifies eligibility for part-time IAs and allows flexible changes of fee mode.
  - Source: [Sansa Legal](https://www.sansalegal.com/post/sebi-investment-advisers-second-amendment-regulations-2025-deposit-system-fee-caps-and-ai-disclos); [AZB & Partners, 3 Feb 2025](https://www.azbpartners.com/bank/overhaul-of-the-regulatory-framework-for-investment-advisers-and-research-analysts/)
- **Responsibility for AI output:** under the 8 Jan 2025 guidelines, IAs and RAs are solely responsible for the security of client data, and for advice or research based on AI-tool output, whether they built or bought the tool. — [TaxGuru](https://taxguru.in/sebi/sebi-eases-rules-investment-advisers-research-analysts-2024-overhaul.html); [SEBI consultation, June 2025, citing the Dec 2024 amendment](https://www.sebi.gov.in/sebi_data/attachdocs/jun-2025/1750415065695.pdf)

**Fee limits**
- Fixed-fee mode is capped at Rs 1,51,000 per year per family of clients, across all IA services.
- An IA may change a client's fee mode at any time.
- The overall maximum is the higher of the fixed-fee cap or 2.5% of AUA per year per family.
- This was reported as part of a SEBI circular of 30 Oct 2025. It was **not** in the Dec 2024 regulation text, and it needs checking against the current IA Master Circular.
- Source: [ELP Law, 20 Nov 2025 (AUA-based fees for second opinion)](https://elplaw.in/leadership/sebi-permits-investment-advisers-to-charge-aua-based-fees-for-a-second-opinion/); search-result summary
- SEBI separately allowed IAs and RAs to charge advance fees for up to one year. — [Business Standard, 2 Apr 2025](https://www.business-standard.com/amp/markets/news/sebi-allows-investment-advisers-analysts-to-charge-advance-fees-for-a-year-125040201085_1.html)

**RA (Third Amendment) Regulations 2024 (notified 16 Dec 2024)**
- The amendment defines "part-time research analyst".
- It lowers the eligibility bar to a graduate degree (previously post-graduate).
- New Reg 19A requires a functional website with details specified by SEBI.
- Source: [SCC Online, 19 Dec 2024](https://www.scconline.com/blog/post/2024/12/19/sebi-research-analyst-third-amendment-regulations-2024/); [TaxGuru](https://taxguru.in/sebi/sebi-updates-regulations-research-analysts-2025.html)
- **AI disclosure (RA side):** new Reg 19(vii) requires an RA to disclose to the client the extent to which it uses AI tools in research services. The disclosure must be made when the terms and conditions are shared. — [SCC Online](https://www.scconline.com/blog/post/2024/12/19/sebi-research-analyst-third-amendment-regulations-2024/)
- SEBI's own model-portfolio regulations were reported as still awaited. — [TaxGuru](https://taxguru.in/sebi/sebi-eases-rules-investment-advisers-research-analysts-2025.html)

**Commentary on "education" labels**
- In one law firm's reading, a course that gives specific buy/sell signals or trading recommendations is investment advice. Calling it "for educational purposes" does not exempt it from registration. — [Aarna Law](https://www.aarnalaw.com/insights/sebis-new-digital-compliance-rules-what-investment-advisers-must-know-in-2026)
- A vendor blog, which is a commercial and lower-weight source, says execution platforms have added AI fund recommendations and model portfolios without holding an advisory licence. — [Genvest](https://www.genvest.ai/blog/ai-wealth-advisor-india-guide)

### Inferences
- **What is likely outside IA/RA scope:**
  - Factual aggregation of the user's own holdings.
  - Arithmetic analytics: allocation, XIRR, concentration percentages, cost and tax illustrations.
  - Generic explanations of concepts.
- **What likely crosses into IA scope:** any output that says, or strongly implies, "you should buy, sell, hold, switch or rebalance X" for this user.
- **What likely crosses into RA scope:** any output that gives a view, rating, target price or recommendation on a listed security.
- **Holding out:** "explains, not advises" copy will not protect Nivara AI if the actual outputs are personalised suggestions, because SEBI looks at substance.
- **Mass-media exclusion:** it is unlikely to cover personalised chat inside an app. That exclusion is aimed at broadcast content widely available to the public.
- **If Nivara AI ever wants to give personalised guidance, the routes are:**
  - Register as a non-individual IA.
  - Operate under a registered IA or RA partner who signs off on content. That partner then carries AI-disclosure duties and sole responsibility for the AI output.
  - A part-time IA registration (75-client cap) may suit a founder-led pilot.
- **[UNVERIFIED]:** RA Regulations define "research report" and "research analyst" broadly enough to cover anyone who prepares or publishes analysis or recommendations on listed securities to the public. That would make RA registration the relevant risk for generic, non-personalised stock opinions given to all users.

- SEBI's January 2025 RA guidelines were not read in full: the circular text on sebi.gov.in could not be fetched.

- No SEBI order or FAQ was found that deals specifically with portfolio-analytics apps or AI explainers.

## 2. Finfluencer and unregistered-entity rules (Aug 2024), the Jan 2025 price-data restriction, and the SEBI AI/ML guidelines

### Takeaway
**August 2024 rule (final):** SEBI-regulated entities and their agents may have no direct or indirect association with anyone who gives securities advice without registration, or who makes return or performance claims.

**January 2025 circular (final):** education providers may use only price data that is at least three months old, and must not use stock names or codes in a way that suggests advice.

**AI/ML guidelines:** these are still only a June 2025 **consultation**. As of mid-to-late November 2026, the SEBI Chairman said final guidelines would be issued "soon". **I found no final circular.**

### Cited Findings

**August 2024 amendments (final)**
- SEBI notified the SEBI (Intermediaries) (Amendment) Regulations 2024, with parallel amendments for depository participants and the SCRR. The notification is dated 26 Aug 2024 and was published around 29 Aug 2024. — [Taxmann](https://www.taxmann.com/post/blog/sebi-amends-intermediaries-regulations-bars-intermediaries-from-associating-with-unregistered-security-advisors); [Outlook Business](https://www.outlookbusiness.com/markets/sebi-amends-rules-to-regulate-finfluencers)
- The amendment inserted a new Chapter IIIA, "restrictions on dealing with other entities". — [Taxmann](https://www.taxmann.com/post/blog/sebi-amends-intermediaries-regulations-bars-intermediaries-from-associating-with-unregistered-security-advisors)
- Under it, no SEBI-regulated person, or agent of one, may have any direct or indirect association with a person who gives investment advice or recommendations without registration, or who makes return or performance claims. — [Upstox](https://upstox.com/news/market-news/financial-regulations/sebi-tightens-rules-for-unregistered-finfluencers-bars-registered-entities-from-dealing-with-them/article-113724); [Legal500 / Mansukhlal Hiralal](https://www.legal500.com/developments/?p=48742)
- SEBI reportedly had content from more than 15,000 unregulated entities removed. — [Business Today, 31 Aug 2024](https://www.businesstoday.in/personal-finance/investment/story/sebi-tweaks-regulations-for-finfluencers-removes-content-of-over-15000-unregulated-entities-443789-2024-08-31)
- A circular of 22 Oct 2024 operationalised the restrictions. — [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/markets/news/sebi-finfluencer-circular-live-stock-data-market-education-rules-125013000571_1.html)

**29 Jan 2025 circular (final)**
- Gave intermediaries three months to sever existing ties with unauthorised advisers and finfluencers. — [YourStory, Jan 2025](https://yourstory.com/2025/01/sebi-tightens-grip-on-unregistered-financial-advisors)
- Stock-market educators cannot use live prices. They may use only data with a three-month lag. — [Business Standard, 30 Jan 2025](https://www.business-standard.com/markets/news/sebi-finfluencer-circular-live-stock-data-market-education-rules-125013000571_1.html); [Business Today](https://businesstoday.in/markets/stocks/story/sebis-clampdown-on-finfluencers-cannot-use-live-stock-market-data-in-educational-content-462704-2025-01-31)
- Educators must not discuss or display stock names, codes or price data from the past three months in any way that could suggest investment advice. This covers videos, speeches and screen shares that might suggest future price movements or recommendations. — [Exchange4media](https://www.exchange4media.com/influence-zone-news/sebi-restricts-finfluencers-from-using-live-stock-market-data-educational-content-140505.html); [Storyboard18](https://www.storyboard18.com/amp/social-media/sebi-cracks-down-on-finfluencers-with-new-rules-on-stock-market-education-55026.htm)
- Education itself is not banned. Providers must not give advice without registration, and must not make performance claims. — [News On Air, 30 Jan 2025](https://www.newsonair.gov.in/sebi-cracks-down-on-finfluencers-selling-stock-tips-in-the-name-of-education); [FoxMandal](https://foxmandal.in/news/cutting-ties-with-finfluencers-sebi-distinguishes-between-education-advice/)

**SEBI AI/ML guidelines (CONSULTATION only, as far as I could find)**
- SEBI published the consultation paper "Guidelines for responsible usage of AI/ML in Indian securities markets" on 20 Jun 2025. Comments were due by 11 Jul 2025. — [SEBI consultation PDF](https://www.sebi.gov.in/sebi_data/attachdocs/jun-2025/1750415065695.pdf); [TaxGuru](https://taxguru.in/sebi/sebi-proposes-guidelines-responsible-usage-ai-ml-indian-securities-markets.html)
- **Main proposals:**
  - AI and ML services supplied by third-party vendors are deemed to be provided by the market participant.
  - Senior management is designated as accountable, with an internal oversight team.
  - Models should be explainable or interpretable.
  - Human review of AI outputs.
  - Testing in a segregated environment before deployment.
  - Source: [Lexology](https://www.lexology.com/library/detail.aspx?g=1ad14350-2973-4596-8e27-b4458dc6c039); [FoxMandal](https://foxmandal.in/News/sebi-floats-paper-on-responsible-ai-use-in-indian-securities-market/)
- **Nov 2026 status:** SEBI Chairman Tuhin Kanta Pandey said at the India International Trade Fair that detailed guidelines on responsible AI use in capital markets would be issued soon. He said they would focus on investor protection, accountability, and mandatory human oversight of major market-related decisions. — [MediaNama, Nov 2026](https://www.medianama.com/2026/11/223-sebi-ai-disclosure-human-oversight-rules/); [ETV Bharat, ~16 Nov 2026](https://www.etvbharat.com/en/business/balancing-tech-and-trust-detailed-view-of-sebis-upcoming-ai-guidelines-for-capital-markets-enn26111601854). Both are known only from search snippets; the pages could not be fetched.
- **AI enforcement:** SEBI says it uses AI for real-time tracking of finfluencers who give illegal investment advice. — [The Tribune](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/ai/using-ai-for-real-time-tracking-of-financial-influencers-giving-illegal-investment-advice-sebi-chief); [Kotak Neo](https://www.kotakneo.com/news/market-news/sebi-ai-surveillance-financialinfluencers-market-oversight/)

### Inferences
- **Brokers and AMCs are SEBI-regulated:** Groww, Motilal Oswal, AMCs and depository participants are all bound by Chapter IIIA. They may refuse to partner with, or cut off API access to, an unregistered app if its content looks like unregistered advice or makes return claims. This is a commercial risk to Nivara AI's broker integrations, separate from any direct SEBI action.
- **Price data:** the three-month rule applies to "education" content. Showing a user their own holdings at live prices is portfolio reporting, not educational content. But if Nia gives "educational" commentary on named stocks at current prices, that sits close to the conduct SEBI is targeting.
- **No return claims:** do not show or advertise "returns achieved" or projected returns as a claim about the app. Goal projections should be framed as user-input illustrations.
- **Design to the AI consultation now:** once final guidelines issue, a registered partner IA or RA, and the brokers, will likely expect Nivara AI to show human oversight, testing, explainability and vendor accountability.

### Gaps
- No final SEBI AI/ML circular was found as of Nov 2026. If one issued in the last few weeks, it was not captured.
- The exact regulation numbers in Chapter IIIA were not confirmed from the gazette.
- No SEBI clarification was found on whether showing users their own holdings at live prices with "explanations" counts as "education" under the Jan 2025 circular.

## 3. SEBI retail algo trading and broker API framework

### Takeaway
**The circular:** "Safer participation of retail investors in Algorithmic trading", SEBI/HO/MIRSD/MIRSD-PoD/P/CIR/2025/0000013, dated 4 Feb 2025 (final). It requires brokers to:
- shut open APIs;
- allow access only through a unique vendor-client API key and a whitelisted static IP;
- register algos with the exchanges.

**Timeline:** the original 1 Aug 2025 start moved to 1 Oct 2025, then to a glide path with full application on 1 Apr 2026, under circular SEBI/HO/MIRSD/MIRSD-PoD/P/CIR/2025/132 of 30 Sep 2025.

**Effect on Nivara AI:** the framework is aimed at order placement. A read-only holdings app is less directly affected, but broker API terms have been tightened as a result.

### Cited Findings
- The circular was issued 4 Feb 2025 and was originally effective 1 Aug 2025. It was extended to 1 Oct 2025 by a circular of 29 Jul 2025. — [Copy of circular (CSE)](https://www.cse-india.com/upload/upload/Feb_042025.pdf); [Angel One](https://www.angelone.in/news/market-updates/sebi-pushes-algo-trading-deadline-to-october-1-2025)
- The 30 Sep 2025 circular (CIR/2025/132) set a glide path, with full applicability to all brokers from 1 Apr 2026. — [SEBI circular PDF](https://www.sebi.gov.in/sebi_data/attachdocs/sep-2025/1759232056254.pdf) (could not be fetched); [AlgoBulls](https://algobulls.com/blog/industry-insights-and-updates/sebi-new-algotrading-regulations-for-retail-investors-2026)
- **Glide-path milestones:**
  - Brokers had to apply to register retail algo products by 31 Oct 2025.
  - Registration had to be complete by 30 Nov 2025.
  - Brokers missing these milestones are barred from onboarding new retail API-algo clients from 5 Jan 2026.
  - Source: [BSE notice of 24 Nov 2025 (mirror)](https://rhnvrm.github.io/stock-market-circulars/circulars/bse/2025/bse-2025-11-24-e6eac9e12bd239db-adherence-to-milestones-and-the-rules-related-to-retail-algo/)
- **API and static IP:** brokers must not permit open APIs. Access is only through a unique vendor-client API key and a static IP whitelisted by the broker. Under NSE implementation standards, retail API users give one or two static IPs. — [AlgoBulls](https://algobulls.com/blog/industry-insights-and-updates/sebi-new-algotrading-regulations-for-retail-investors-2026); [TradeSteady](https://www.tradesteadyalgo.com/pages/sebi-algo-trading-rules-2026.html)
- Sources disagree on what happened in practice. Some brokers went live on 1 Oct 2025, while other sources present 1 Apr 2026 as the date static IP and API order tagging became mandatory. — [AlphaQuark](https://alphaquark.in/blog/sebi-retail-algo-trading-rules-2026-guide); [Sahi](https://www.sahi.com/blogs/sebi-algo-trading-rules-2026-what-every-retail-trader-must-know-before-april)

### Inferences
- **[UNVERIFIED, from background knowledge of the 4 Feb 2025 circular]:**
  - Algo providers ("vendors") must be empanelled with the exchanges as agents of the broker.
  - Brokers remain the principal and are responsible.
  - API sessions use OAuth-based authentication with two-factor authentication, and log out before the next trading session.
  - Orders above an exchange-set order-per-second threshold (reported as 10 orders/sec) must be registered as algos and tagged with an exchange algo ID.
  - Clients below that threshold who build their own algos still use static IP.
  - Check all of this against the circular and the NSE/BSE implementation standards.
- **Read-only access:** if Nivara AI only reads holdings and positions through broker APIs (or, preferably, through Account Aggregator or depository data) and never places orders, it should fall outside algo registration and vendor empanelment.
- **But broker terms still bind:** broker API terms now commonly limit third-party apps, require per-user OAuth consent, forbid credential sharing, and may require the static IP of the app's servers. Nivara AI should get written partner agreements with each broker instead of relying on retail-developer API keys. Retail keys are meant for the client's own personal use, and pooling them for a multi-user app is likely a breach of broker terms.
- **Never add a "place order" button.** Doing so would turn Nivara AI into an algo or third-party execution vendor needing empanelment. It would also strengthen the argument that the app is giving advice.

### Gaps
- Groww's and Motilal Oswal's current terms for third-party apps and read-only data access in 2026 were not found.
- The text of the exchange implementation standards on read-only API use was not verified.

## 4. RBI Account Aggregator framework: can Nivara AI become an FIU?

### Takeaway
**Short answer: no, not directly.** Under the RBI NBFC-AA Master Directions (2016, as amended), an FIU must be an entity registered with and regulated by a financial-sector regulator (RBI, SEBI, IRDAI or PFRDA), using the AA for a purpose its licence permits. An unregistered app cannot become an FIU on its own.

**Routes in:**
- Get registered, most plausibly as a SEBI IA or RA.
- Partner with a regulated FIU, such as a registered IA, NBFC or broker, and operate under its licence and purpose code.

**Data available through AA:** depositories (NSDL, CDSL) and MF RTAs (CAMS, KFin) are live FIPs that share demat and MF holdings.

### Cited Findings
- SEBI's August 2022 circular brought depositories into the AA framework as FIPs. FIUs are defined as entities registered with and regulated by a financial-sector regulator. — [NSDL FIP page](https://nsdl.com/resources/fip); [SEBI consultation, Aug 2023](https://www.sebi.gov.in/sebi_data/attachdocs/aug-2023/1690887848711.pdf)
- **CDSL** has been live as an FIP since April 2023. It shares equities, MF units, ETFs, IDRs and CIS. Users link their demat account on the AA using the mobile number and PAN registered with it, plus an OTP. — [CDSL FIP page](https://www.cdslindia.com/Investors/FIP.html)
- **NSDL** shares demat holdings with FIUs on the basis of consent. — [NSDL](https://nsdl.com/financial-information-provider)
- **RTAs** CAMS and KFin share MF units and SIP data. — [Sahamati: FI types activated by non-bank FIPs](https://sahamati.org.in/fi-types-activated-by-non-banking-fips-on-aa/)
- **Reciprocity:** Sahamati says an entity is generally expected to be an FIP before it can be an FIU, and its purpose must match its licensed charter. — [Sahamati FAQ](https://sahamati.org.in/faq/)
- **SEBI consultation of 1 Aug 2023:** it proposed defining FIU use cases for SEBI-regulated entities. Examples include an RIA obtaining a client's financial-asset information, PMS, and bank-account verification at onboarding. — [SEBI consultation](https://www.sebi.gov.in/sebi_data/attachdocs/aug-2023/1690887848711.pdf); [Business Standard](https://business-standard.com/amp/markets/news/sebi-floats-consultation-paper-to-improve-account-aggregator-framework-123080100740_1.html)
- Sahamati's response flagged purpose-code mapping problems for SEBI categories, including overlap between codes 102 and 104. — [Sahamati response PDF](https://sahamati.org.in/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/Sahamatis-response-to-SEBI-consultation-paper-August-2023.pdf)
- **Adoption:** only 193 of 9,120 SEBI-regulated entities had joined the AA ecosystem as of Dec 2024. — [Sahamati adoption update, 31 Dec 2024](https://sahamati.org.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Account-Aggregator-Adoption-update-31st-Dec-2024.pdf)
- A May 2026 news item says SEBI extended the comment period on an Account Aggregator proposal. This suggests a newer SEBI AA consultation exists, but its content could not be confirmed. — [Telangana Today, May 2026](https://telanganatoday.com/sebi-extends-timeline-to-submit-public-comments-on-proposal-concerning-account-aggregator)

### Inferences
- **Most practical route to AA data for Nivara AI:** become a SEBI-registered IA, which the 2023 SEBI paper treats as a natural FIU use case, or work with a regulated FIU partner.
- **Alternatives without a licence:** CAS (Consolidated Account Statement) PDF upload or email parsing, and broker OAuth APIs. These still need DPDP-grade consent.
- **[UNVERIFIED]:**
  - EPF (EPFO) and NPS (CRAs, PFRDA) data through AA had limited or patchy live availability as of the last reliable reporting.
  - NPS and insurance FIP readiness under PFRDA and IRDAI has historically lagged.
  - Check current FIP lists on Sahamati.

### Gaps
- The final outcome of SEBI's 2023 FIU consultation, and the 2026 AA proposal, are unknown.
- Current live status of EPF and NPS as FIPs was not verified.
- rbi.org.in was not fetched for the current Master Directions text.

## 5. DPDP Act 2023 and DPDP Rules 2025

### Takeaway
**The Rules are final.** They were notified in November 2025 (13 or 14 Nov; sources differ), G.S.R. 846(E), and take effect in phases:
- Data Protection Board provisions: immediately on notification.
- Consent Manager registration: from about 13 Nov 2026, so **now live**.
- Most fiduciary duties (notice, security safeguards, breach intimation, retention and erasure, children's data): from about 13 May 2027.

Nivara AI handles financial data and will be a Data Fiduciary. It should build to the May 2027 obligations now.

### Cited Findings
- **Timeline:**
  - Notified 13 Nov 2025 as G.S.R. 846(E).
  - Data Protection Board operational from notification.
  - Consent Manager provisions from 13 Nov 2026.
  - Main duties from 13 May 2027: Rules 3, 5–16, 22 and 23 commence 18 months after notification.
  - Source: [TCSA](https://www.tcsa.in/resources/dpdp-rules-2025-implementation-roadmap); [Legismith](https://legismith.com/dpdp-rules-2025-timeline/); [AMS Shardul alert](https://www.amsshardul.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Regulatory-Alert-Enforcement-of-DPDP-Act-and-Notification-of-DPDP-Rules.pdf)
  - Some sources date notification to 14 Nov 2025. — [Takshashila](https://takshashila.org.in/content/blogs/20251114-meity-notifies-dpdp-rules-2025.html)
- **Breach reporting (Rule 7):**
  - Intimation to the Board and to affected Data Principals without delay.
  - A detailed report to the Board within 72 hours.
  - Secondary sources differ on exactly who must receive what within 72 hours.
  - Source: [Jurocompliant](https://jurocompliant.com/blog/dpdp-rules-2025-explained); [AMLegals](https://amlegals.in/dpdp-rules-2025)
- **Children (Rule 10):**
  - Verifiable parental consent is required before processing personal data of anyone under 18, using appropriate technical and organisational measures.
  - Some fiduciaries, such as healthcare and educational institutions, are exempt for specified purposes.
  - Source: [LiveLaw](https://www.livelaw.in/amp/top-stories/digital-personal-data-protection-rules-2025-notified-309928); [SCC Online, 26 Dec 2025](https://www.scconline.com/blog/post/2025/12/26/digital-personal-data-protection-rules-2025-key-highlights/)
- **Cross-border transfer:** permitted unless the Central Government restricts it. Rules also allow conditions to be imposed, particularly on Significant Data Fiduciaries. Sources differ on the rule number (13 vs 15). — [India Briefing](https://www.india-briefing.com/news/dpdp-rules-2025-india-data-protection-law-compliance-40769.html/); [Hogan Lovells](https://www.hoganlovells.com/en/publications/indias-digital-personal-data-protection-act-2023-brought-into-force-)
- **Significant Data Fiduciaries:** extra duties include DPIAs, annual audits, algorithmic due-diligence, and possible localisation of specified data. — [EY](https://ey.com/en_in/insights/cybersecurity/transforming-data-privacy-digital-personal-data-protection-rules-2025)
- **Retention:** the notified Rules added a data-retention obligation that was not in the draft. — [Mondaq: 8 key takeaways](https://webiis10.mondaq.com/india/privacy-protection/1709084/8-key-takeaways-the-digital-personal-data-protection-rules-2025)

### Inferences
- **[UNVERIFIED, from background knowledge]:**
  - The final Rules require fiduciaries to keep personal data, traffic data and processing logs for at least one year for Board and government purposes.
  - The three-year inactivity erasure (with 48-hour prior notice) applies to specified large e-commerce, gaming and social-media intermediaries (2 crore or more users), not to a small fintech.
  - Act penalties go up to Rs 250 crore for failing to maintain security safeguards, and up to Rs 200 crore for failing to notify a breach.
  - Check these in the gazette.
- **Consent Managers:** registered Consent Managers (Board-registered, India-incorporated, with a minimum net worth reported at Rs 2 crore [UNVERIFIED]) are now possible. RBI-licensed Account Aggregators already work as consent managers for financial data. Using AA consent artefacts would line Nivara AI up with both regimes.
- **Practical duties:**
  - A purpose-specific, itemised consent notice in English or an Eighth Schedule language.
  - Withdrawal as easy as giving consent.
  - A grievance officer or DPO contact.
  - Age-gating: block users under 18, or get verifiable parental consent.
  - Contracts with LLM and cloud processors, including where data goes for any foreign LLM API.
  - Avoid sending PAN, account numbers or full holdings to third-party LLMs unless the user has consented and the processor contract covers it.

## 6. CERT-In directions and SEBI CSCRF

### Takeaway
**CERT-In Directions** (28 Apr 2022, under s.70B(6) of the IT Act, effective 27 Jun 2022) apply to service providers, intermediaries, data centres and body corporates. That includes Nivara AI. They require:
- reporting specified cyber incidents within **6 hours**;
- keeping ICT logs for a rolling **180 days** in India;
- NTP time synchronisation;
- a designated point of contact.

**SEBI CSCRF** (Aug 2024) applies only to SEBI-regulated entities. It applies to Nivara AI only if it registers (for example as an IA), or indirectly through broker or partner contracts.

### Cited Findings
- CERT-In Directions were issued 28 Apr 2022 under s.70B(6) and became effective 27 Jun 2022. — [Khaitan & Co ERGO, 30 Apr 2022](https://khaitanco.com/sites/default/files/2022-04/Ergo_30042022.pdf); [AZB](https://www.azbpartners.com/bank/cert-in-directions/)
- **6-hour reporting:** incidents must be reported within 6 hours of being noticed. If full information is not available, partial information may be sent within 6 hours, with the rest following within a reasonable time. — [Mondaq](https://www.mondaq.com/india/it-and-internet/1270362/6-hours-to-report-cyber-incidents-to-cert-in-and-more-compliances); [Linklaters](https://www.linklaters.com/en/insights/blogs/digilinks/2022/may/india-new-and-onerous-cyber-security-framework-and-breach-reporting-obligations)
- **Logs:** 180 days rolling. Per the FAQs, logs may be stored abroad only if a copy is kept in India. — [Lakshmikumaran & Sridharan](https://lakshmisri.com/insights/articles/analyzing-the-new-cert-in-directions-wider-gamut-than-breach-reporting)
- **CSCRF:** issued Aug 2024, with clarifications in Dec 2024. It applies to 19 categories of SEBI-regulated entities. Compliance deadlines were extended several times: originally 1 Jan / 1 Apr 2025, then 31 Mar 2025, then 30 Jun 2025, then reportedly 31 Aug 2025 for some entities. — [5paisa](https://www.5paisa.com/news/sebi-extends-cybersecurity-framework-deadline-for-regulated-entities-to-august-31-2025); [Angel One](https://www.angelone.in/blog/sebi-extends-deadline-for-cybersecurity-framework-compliance-to-june-30-2025); [Deloitte](https://www2.deloitte.com/content/dam/Deloitte/in/Documents/tax/in-tax-gbt-clarifications-to-cybersecurity-and-cyber-resilience-framework-noexp.pdf)

### Inferences
- **One incident-response runbook should cover three regimes:**
  - CERT-In: 6 hours.
  - DPDP: Board and users without delay, then 72 hours (from May 2027).
  - Broker or partner contractual notice, which brokers will push down because CSCRF binds them.
- **If Nivara AI registers as an IA,** CSCRF applies under the category for small or self-certification REs [UNVERIFIED tiering]. That means SOC, VAPT and audit obligations scaled to its size.

## 7. Tax-illustration disclaimers and dark-pattern rules

### Takeaway
**Dark patterns:** the CCPA's Guidelines for Prevention and Regulation of Dark Patterns 2023 (Nov 2023, under s.18 of the Consumer Protection Act 2019) list 13 prohibited patterns and apply to platforms offering goods or services in India. A June 2025 advisory asked e-commerce platforms to self-audit within 3 months.

**Tax illustrations:** no India-specific rule was found. The prudent approach is to:
- label them as illustrative;
- base them on stated assumptions and the current Finance Act rates;
- state that they are not tax advice or tax-filing advice.

### Cited Findings
- The CCPA issued the Guidelines in Nov 2023 under s.18 of the Consumer Protection Act 2019. Annexure I lists 13 patterns, including false urgency, basket sneaking and confirm-shaming. They also cover foreign platforms serving India. — [Mondaq](https://www.mondaq.com/india/advertising-marketing-branding/1400996/guidelines-notified-for-prevention-and-regulation-of-dark-patterns); [Deloitte](https://www2.deloitte.com/in/en/pages/tax/articles/tax-alert-the-guidelines-for-prevention-and-regulation-of-dark-patterns-2023.html)
- **5 Jun 2025 advisory:** e-commerce platforms should self-audit for dark patterns within 3 months and give self-declarations. — [Exchange4media](https://www.exchange4media.com/marketing-news/ccpa-gives-ecomm-players-3-months-to-root-out-dark-patterns-144202.html); [FoxMandal](https://foxmandal.in/news/dark-patterns-back-in-spotlight-as-new-advisory-calls-for-self-audits/)
- The advisory itself is described as non-binding, with no stated penalties. Experts say repeated violations could be penalised under the Consumer Protection Act. — [Bar & Bench](https://www.barandbench.com/law-firms/view-point/dark-patterns-in-india-from-hidden-practices-to-regulatory-spotlight); [IAPP](https://iapp.org/news/a/india-s-ccpa-guidelines-on-dark-patterns-welcome-signal-but-law-is-still-soft)

### Inferences
- **Patterns relevant to Nivara AI's design:**
  - Subscription traps (hard-to-cancel premium plans).
  - Drip pricing.
  - Nagging.
  - Interface interference: pre-ticked consent boxes, which also conflict with DPDP's requirement for affirmative consent.
  - False urgency on broker-switch prompts.
- **Broker-cost comparisons:** keep them neutral, with no undisclosed referral fees. Disclose any affiliate or referral payment from a broker. An undisclosed referral could also be read as an "association" problem for the broker under Chapter IIIA.
- **Tax illustrations should state:**
  - the assessment year and the rules applied, for example LTCG on listed equity under s.112A and STCG under s.111A at post-23 Jul 2024 rates [UNVERIFIED rates; check the current Finance Act];
  - that grandfathering, set-offs, surcharge and residential status are simplified;
  - that users should consult a CA.

### Gaps

- No confirmation was found on whether CCPA enforcement on dark patterns has extended to fintech apps by 2026.

## 8. Practical product guardrails used by Indian fintechs, and recommended design choices

### Takeaway
The lowest-risk design for an unregistered Nivara AI is:
- factual aggregation and analytics of the user's own data;
- generic, security-agnostic education;
- no buy, sell, hold or switch language, no target prices, no ratings, no named-stock picks;
- no return claims;
- user-driven what-if illustrations with visible assumptions;
- AI-use disclosure and human-reviewed content policies, modelled on SEBI's IA/RA AI rules and the 2025 AI consultation.

Anything personalised should go through a SEBI-registered IA or RA partner, or Nivara AI's own registration.

### Cited Findings
- Labelling content "educational" does not avoid registration if it contains specific buy or sell recommendations. — [Aarna Law](https://www.aarnalaw.com/insights/sebis-new-digital-compliance-rules-what-investment-advisers-must-know-in-2026)
- The exemptions (general comments naming no security; mass-media content) are the only clear safe harbours. — [ICMAI copy of IA Regs](https://icmai.in/upload/pd/SEBI-IA-Regulations-2013.pdf)
- Registered IAs and RAs must disclose AI tools, stages of use and limitations, and are solely accountable for AI output. — [Sansa Legal](https://www.sansalegal.com/post/sebi-investment-advisers-second-amendment-regulations-2025-deposit-system-fee-caps-and-ai-disclos); [SCC Online](https://www.scconline.com/blog/post/2024/12/19/sebi-research-analyst-third-amendment-regulations-2024/)
- Educators may not use live or recent price data, or name stocks, in ways that suggest advice. — [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/markets/news/sebi-finfluencer-circular-live-stock-data-market-education-rules-125013000571_1.html)
- The proposed AI guidelines call for human oversight, explainability, pre-deployment testing and vendor accountability. — [SEBI consultation, Jun 2025](https://www.sebi.gov.in/sebi_data/attachdocs/jun-2025/1750415065695.pdf)

### Inferences (design recommendations; validate with counsel)

**1. Nia output policy**
- Hard-block imperative or recommendation language: "buy", "sell", "switch to", "you should", "best fund for you".
- Block target prices, ratings, forecasts of a security's price, and stock or fund picks.
- When a user asks "what should I do?", Nia should:
  - decline politely;
  - explain the trade-offs generically;
  - route the user to a SEBI-registered IA (partner or directory), with a link to SEBI's intermediary search.
- Log prompts and outputs. Red-team regularly and keep test records.

**2. Separate facts from interpretation**
- Analytics (allocation, concentration, expense ratios, broker costs, tax arithmetic) are computed deterministically and shown with formulas.
- Nia explains what a metric means, not what to do about it.
- Avoid comparisons that rank specific funds or brokers as "better for you".

**3. Projections**
- Goal projections use user-chosen return assumptions and show ranges or scenarios.
- No guaranteed or "expected" returns. No past-performance marketing claims, consistent with the "return claims" restriction.

**4. Disclosures**
- State on every screen and in the chat that Nivara AI is not SEBI-registered as an IA or RA.
- State that content is information and education, not investment, tax or legal advice.
- Include an AI disclosure: the model is AI, may be wrong, its limitations, and data use (who processes data and where).
- Tax illustrations should be dated and state the law version applied.

**5. Data access**
- Prefer AA (through a regulated FIU partner) or CAS upload over broker credential handling.
- Where broker APIs are used, get formal partner agreements with OAuth per-user consent and read-only scopes.
- Never store broker passwords or TOTP secrets, and never place orders. This keeps Nivara AI outside the Feb 2025 algo or vendor regime.

**6. DPDP readiness for May 2027**
- Consent notice, ability to withdraw consent, retention schedule, breach runbook (CERT-In 6h, DPDP 72h), age gate for users under 18.
- DPAs with LLM and cloud vendors, minimising PII sent to LLMs.
- Keep logs for 180 days or more in India.

**7. Regulatory pathways**
- A part-time or non-individual IA registration, or partnership with a registered IA or RA, before any personalised features.
- SEBI's Innovation Sandbox or Regulatory Sandbox [UNVERIFIED current eligibility and requirements; SEBI's regulatory sandbox framework was revised around 2023–2025, so confirm the latest circular].
- RBI's Regulatory Sandbox only if Nivara AI moves into payments or lending.

**8. Marketing**
- No finfluencer tie-ups where the influencer gives stock tips.
- No referral arrangements that could make a regulated broker "associated" with unregistered advice.
- No dark patterns: easy cancellation, no pre-ticked consents.

- No SEBI enforcement order against an AI portfolio-explainer app was found that would test where the line sits.
- Current SEBI sandbox circular details were not verified.
